Warehouses and player trade

All clan members can deposit. Only the leader and two co-leaders can withdraw. Augmented equipment cannot be deposited into the clan warehouse. Personal storage keeps its enchantment, SA, augmentation and attributes. Click an item to enter its quantity in a popup. Trades exchange up to 12 stacks per side and require both players to confirm the same offer.
