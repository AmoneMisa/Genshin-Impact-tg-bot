# CoL top-up artwork

Generated with the built-in image_gen tool, one transparent raster image per pack.
Original PNGs remain here; the UI consumes 128px/256px WebP images in `webapp/art/col-topup`.
Re-encode with `scripts/ui/encode-col-packs.py` (Pillow).

## Prompt specification

Create one production-ready raster fantasy RPG item icon: SUBJECT. Isolated on fully transparent background, single centered object, full object visible with generous empty padding, square composition. Golden Coin of Luck currency only: round gold coins bearing a simple embossed four-leaf clover, no gems, no crystals, no jewels, no blue magical energy. Painterly detailed medieval Lineage II aesthetic, warm realistic metallic highlights and brown leather/wood, readable silhouette at 96 pixels, three-quarter view. NO text, letters, numerals, border, frame, UI, background, watermark, emoji or vector art.

## Subjects

- pouch: a small loose pile of 5 golden coins
- sack: a soft small dark leather drawstring coin pouch with golden coins spilling out
- casket: a small exquisite golden wooden jewelry casket open and filled with golden coins
- chest: an open sturdy iron-bound treasure chest filled with golden coins
- trove: a large ornate royal gold-bound treasure chest overflowing with golden coins and coin piles beside it
- vault: a magnificent compact golden treasure vault overflowing with abundant gold coin stacks
