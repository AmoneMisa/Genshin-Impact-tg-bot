# Early hunting environment references

Source URLs are recorded in `sources.json`. Screenshots were inspected on 2026-10-16; the exact capture builds are not identified. They are environment references, not verified High Five captures, runtime backgrounds or authoritative geodata. The Talking Island reference depicts Eastern Territory, not the post-Awakening rebuilt island.

Observed features:

- Talking Island: gentle grass and brown dirt, broadleaf trees mixed with conifers, small stone-and-timber house and low stone fence.
- Elven Forest: green rolling grass, large drooping trees, mossy roots, small ruined grey stone arch.
- Swampland: grey-olive muddy ground, ragged swamp trees and long hanging moss; no red corruption structures.
- The Dark Forest: dense twisted nearly bare trees, grey-brown soil, low rounded rock ledges.
- Orc Barracks: open grassy slopes, wooden skull totems, modest hide huts and rough palisades.
