# City design reference

User-supplied city design: reference.png (offline source, not a runtime background).
The screenshot contains UI and text, so it must not be used as a game background.

Visual target: continuous royal kingdom map; blue mage tower near the center,
giant sword-bearing statue on the left, floating castle on the right, distant peaks
and waterfalls, gold and amber windows, dark ornate gold building plates.
Controls must remain real, accessible UI driven by server building data.
On narrow screens keep recognizable landmarks and generous touch targets;
do not invent extra buildings or costs solely to match the picture.

Completed 8 October 2026: the header panorama and map-portrait.png are reviewed.
The city uses the portrait landscape behind the live building controls, with a
natural-aspect upper scene and a cropped lower landscape beneath the paired lots.
This avoids stretching the statue/castles to fit a long phone page. All seven
buildings have three transparent paintings selected by real building level.
The game retains its own building roster and rules; the design is a visual target,
not an instruction to invent the screenshot's markets or event mechanics.
